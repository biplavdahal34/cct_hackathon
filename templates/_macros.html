How this price is worked out +
  • Every listing a buyer has checked with BEAT is one data point: what the seller asked, and the score and verdict BEAT gave it.
  • The price is the average of those asking prices, with each one counted in proportion to its score. A listing rated Buy worthy at 150,000 counts for far more than one rated Caution at 200,000, so the estimate stays close to 150,000.
  • A listing checked more than once counts once. Listings that BEAT scored very low still count a little, because a bad score is often about the device, not the price.
  • These are asking prices from listings that were checked, not sale prices. Prices collected from the site will be added later to make the estimate more accurate.
