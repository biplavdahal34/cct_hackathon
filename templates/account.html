How your keys are handled +
  • Each key is checked with the provider once when you save it, then stored encrypted. It's decrypted only while one of your own checks is running, and never shown again — only the last four characters.
  • Keys are used only for listings you check yourself. Nothing else on BEAT touches them.
  • Your photos and listing text go to Google under your own key, so Google's terms for your account apply.
  • Create a key just for BEAT. Apify can issue a token limited to running actors; for Gemini, a free AI Studio key with a daily quota set in Google Cloud. Remove it here any time.
  • Your key is also used for the report assistant, proof checks and Nepali translations on your own reports, and nowhere else.
